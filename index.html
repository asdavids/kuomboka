Naa Atswei in Zambia: A Ghanaian Vlogger's Journey Through Culture, Adventure, and Connection

By AS Davids | Kuomboka.com | Culture

Ghanaian travel blogger and vlogger Naa Atswei has taken social media by storm with her inspiring journey across Zambia. Through her series tagged #GhanaianGirlInZambia, she documents not just destinations, but real experiences, emotions, and cultural discoveries.

Experiencing the Kuomboka Ceremony in Western Zambia

One of the most powerful highlights of Naa Atswei's visit was attending the Kuomboka Ceremony, a historic and cultural event of the Lozi people in Western Zambia. The Kuomboka marks the ceremonial movement of the Litunga (Lozi King) from the flooded plains to higher ground.

Victoria Falls and The Boiling Pot

Naa Atswei's journey also took her to Victoria Falls (Mosi-oa-Tunya) in Livingstone, one of the Seven Natural Wonders of the World.

Exploring Zambian Food and Daily Life

Naa didn't just visit tourist attractions — she immersed herself in everyday Zambian life, sharing her experiences with nshima, local chicken dishes, and engaging her audience with recommendations from locals.

Zambia Through Her Eyes: A Story of Gratitude

Naa Atswei's visit to Zambia is more than just a travel vlog — it is a cultural bridge between Ghana and Zambia, reflecting the hospitality of Zambians and the power of shared African experiences.
